Pine

A private home dashboard, used only by one household.

Privacy

Pine runs on a computer in our home and is used only by members of our household. When a household member signs in with Google, Pine uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read and control that household's own Nest thermostats: their temperatures, modes and setpoints.

That information stays on the household's own computer. It is used only to show and adjust the thermostats, and it is never sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. Pine requests no other Google data.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.
